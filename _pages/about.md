## Bio
I am an associate professor (tenure-track) in the [VisGroup](https://vis.uib.no/) at the Department of Informatics, University of Bergen, Norway where I lead the Visual Engagement Lab. I have been active in visualization research since 2018, preceded by several years’ experience as a biomedical illustrator and content director in successful health technology startups across the US. 

I have been active on the organizing and program committees of top visualization venues such as IEEE VIS and EG EuroVIS as well as topic-specific symposia such as EG Visual Computing for Biology and Medicine. In addition, I serve on the Board of the [Vesalius Trust for Visual Communication in the Health Sciences](https://vesaliustrust.org/), a non-profit foundation to support education and research in medical illustration and related fields. 

My visualization research has earned notable awards, including the Dirk Bartz Prize for Visual Computing in Medicine and the Karl-Heinz Höhne (MedVis) Award.

My current research focuses on designing visual tools to support exploration and communication about phenomena in our world, and investigating the impacts of data design strategies on audience/user engagement through alternative theories and epistemologies. Through collaborations with partners in biology, medicine, linguistics, and social sciences, I hope to bridge theory and practice towards a future where we interact with data as a fluid, contextual object.
